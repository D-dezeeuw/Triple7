Welcome to Triple7!

Three little machines, one juicy chain:

  1. Match glass fruit on the board below — every match squeezes Juice, always free.
  2. Feed 7 Juice to the Slots for a spin — wins pay Suncoins.
  3. Drop 7 Suncoins into the Dozer — coins pushed off the edge pay Stargems.

Spend Stargems on charms, upgrades and automation to make the whole loop faster. No ads, no accounts, no purchases — just fruit. (Replay this anytime from Settings.)
